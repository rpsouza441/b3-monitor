## Technical indicators

Proposed v1 registry: SMA20, SMA50, Wilder RSI14, EMA9/21 and finalized daily abnormal volume on a selected compatible completed daily series. Version definition, parameters, seed/convergence policy, decimal arithmetic/rounding, input revisions, effective trading date, required/actual bars and readiness. SMA N requires N eligible closes; daily-close/SMA crossing needs two eligible daily outputs. A sampled quote/SMA crossing needs two eligible comparison pairs and may use the same completed-day SMA if maximum daily-input age and basis gates hold. Version equality convention and maximum sample-gap; changing SMA revision/date or an ineligible interval establishes a fresh baseline unless an explicitly reviewed policy permits comparison. RSI14 first seed requires 14 deltas/15 closes; computability alone is not convergence assurance. Define and test explicit flat/no-loss/no-gain conventions before enabling. Never silently reseed a recursive indicator from a moving three-month window.

Unsupported/insufficient/incompatible/unknown-finality returns NOT_READY with reason. Do not shorten periods or claim intraday candles. Price versus daily SMA is an explicitly sampled mixed-time rule and needs compatible quote/history basis plus a maximum daily-input age.

EMA9/21: alpha=2/(N+1); seed each series with the first N eligible closes' SMA, persist seed/input revision and recursively replay corrections. Warmup/convergence and rounding tolerance require library/version goldens before enablement. EMA9 versus EMA21 trend/crossing compares both outputs for the same completed eligible date/source/basis; observed crossing requires eligible ordered comparison pairs and IND-03 gap/equality rules. An already-above pair follows the selected CROSSING/LEVEL initial policy, not an independent indicator alert. A short series can be computable yet LIMITED_HISTORY; no long-history-equivalence claim or rolling three-month reseed.

Abnormal volume: Vt / mean(Vt-20 … Vt-1), with 21 compatible finalized daily bars, explicit volume unit/security definition, one source/basis and no current-day cumulative comparison. Vt is excluded from denominator; missing/provisional/unknown-unit bars, incompatible corporate-action units, or nonpositive mean make it NOT_READY. COTAHIST close-only cannot provide this operand. Threshold/equality/rearm apply to this ratio, not an investment recommendation.

Volatility is proposed v2 (FUT-05), not delivered silently as another indicator. Its close-to-close return definition, raw/adjusted/total-return basis, window, sample/population deviation, sampling frequency, annualization, action/gap policy and adequate history are unresolved. Human approval must accept this deferral or add a validated v1 requirement.

## Rule scope, composition and input eligibility

Typed bounded DSL with immutable revision, asset ID, operand/comparator/unit/timeframe/threshold, mode and explicit initial-alert policy, hysteresis/false confirmations/cooldown, input-age/skew/gap policy and enable state. No code, SQL or financial-context operands. Rule validity and TRUE/FALSE/UNKNOWN evaluation are distinct. Proposed above/below means strict >/<, equality FALSE; inclusive >=/<= must be explicitly selected/versioned if approved, never inferred from rounding. Compare at declared decimal precision before display rounding; persist comparator and threshold. Rearm false confirmations also require the configured hysteresis boundary, not merely display equality.

Phase 4 enables only single-leaf manual observed-price BRL thresholds (RUL-01–05). It can test both initial-alert modes without expanding to percentage, composition or indicators. Prior-close UNKNOWN need not disable an independently eligible absolute price. Corporate actions/discontinuities quarantine affected targets until reviewed; no automatic rescale.

Phase 5A enables RUL-06 percentage above/below signed percent thresholds and RUL-07 same-asset AND/OR. Percentage uses decimal 100×(price−previousClose)/previousClose, never provider change fields. Require independently verified previous trading-session close date, positive denominator, instrument/currency/unit, source basis and corporate-action compatibility. Calendar inference/field name alone cannot prove previousClose date. If the chosen input cannot supply this evidence, percentage stays disabled; do not invent a substitute from an incompatible series. Day rollover/action/correction invalidates comparison continuity and requires fresh eligible baselining.

Composition: explicit parenthesized typed AST, proposed maximum 8 leaves and depth 4, only approved comparator/unit/timeframe combinations. Cross-asset rules are excluded. At enablement all leaf capabilities must exist and pass their gates. At runtime ANY ineligible/UNKNOWN leaf makes the whole expression UNKNOWN, including OR with a TRUE leaf. This conservative completeness policy differs from permissive three-valued short circuiting and requires human approval. When all leaves are eligible, normal AND/OR truth tables apply. Evaluate under one coherent cutoff, approved per-input age/max skew/timeframe/basis policy and exact all-leaf lineage. Different timeframe use needs an explicitly approved comparison policy; do not equate timestamps or mix raw/adjusted values. Phase 5B adds technical leaves only after HIS/IND gates. Missing history/exporter does not delay eligible price/percentage composition.

## Rule state, initial alerts and deduplication

**Mode comparison (ADR-012, Q-19):** CROSSING sends only an observed eligible FALSE→TRUE transition after arming. An already-true condition at first eligible observation sends nothing until distinct eligible false confirmations rearm it and it subsequently becomes true. This avoids surprise initial alerts but can miss an existing opportunity. LEVEL optionally sends one initial logical alert when the first eligible observation is true; it makes existing conditions visible but can produce an initial burst. It is not repeated level notification. Subsequent episodes in either mode use the same false-confirmation/hysteresis rearm and transition path. Polls can miss brief excursions in either mode.

Default mode is **UNSELECTED**, not implicitly CROSSING. Human must approve the default or select a mode per rule before activation. LEVEL initial_on_create, initial_on_edit and initial_on_resume are explicit opt-ins, default proposals disabled pending approval. CROSSING rejects initial opt-ins. Mode/policy changes create a new immutable revision.

Persist BASELINING/ARMED/TRIGGERED/PAUSED, initial-consumed marker, lifecycle epoch, episode ID, last distinct eligible comparison/false-confirmation IDs, latch, cooldown deadline and policies. UNKNOWN cannot trigger/rearm/consume an initial token or accrue false confirmations; display/record reason. An unconsumed authorized LEVEL initial opportunity waits for the first eligible evaluation; if FALSE, consume the initial opportunity without sending and arm after the configured distinct false confirmations. An already-TRUE LEVEL initial consumes its token and creates one latched episode atomically. Concurrent initial evaluations cannot each allocate an episode.

Rearm uses configured distinct eligible false evaluations (proposed two) and comparator-specific hysteresis, of the whole expression. Duplicate input/evaluation IDs never advance counts. A latched TRUE condition sends once per episode; continued TRUE and cooldown expiry never create another episode. Cooldown is a rule/recipient dispatch limit, preserved across revisions/resume/restart; it can defer an existing fresh intent but cannot renew its expiry or create a signal. If no valid live condition/intent remains at dispatch, suppress or expire with reason; a later observation is a new evaluation, not a replacement lineage for the old signal.

Creation establishes a revision/lifecycle epoch. Edit establishes a new revision, discards stale computations, resets comparison continuity and cancels unsent old-revision intents with audit; already accepted messages cannot be recalled. Explicit LEVEL initial_on_edit can create one new revision's initial episode, subject to carried cooldown/rate limits; editing cannot bypass storm controls. Without this opt-in, require baselining/rearm before a new episode. Pausing stops claims/new evaluations and preserves consumed/latch/episode/cooldown state.

Ordinary resume keeps the initial-consumed marker and latch, starts fresh comparison baselining and never replays an existing TRUE episode. An explicit approved LEVEL initial_on_resume establishes one new initial opportunity only when the prior latched episode has ended under eligible false-confirmation rules; otherwise suppress it. Both mode edits and resume preserve cross-revision cooldown. Neither restart, source gap, date/basis change nor history correction automatically grants another initial token. Ineligible gaps reset comparison continuity/false-confirmation streaks in both modes but preserve latched episode/consumption; reconnect cannot infer a missed crossing.

Logical signal key (rule_id, revision, lifecycle_epoch, episode_id) and recipient key (signal_id, recipient_id) are unique. Evaluation IDs/input hashes and policy versions are immutable. Transaction rechecks revision/state and commits evaluation, token consumption, episode, signal and outbox together. Restore preserves these keys and cooldown. Lost/unreconciled state causes recovery quarantine with collectors/senders disabled, not creation/resume or guessed rearming. Restore never blindly resends possibly accepted intents. Independent submission uncertainty remains governed by the outbox contract.

## Outbox and external delivery

Durable intents have logical key, template revision, recipient route, expiry and event lineage. Claims use leases and fencing; external sends occur after commit. Record attempt/provider message ID. Transport states include PENDING, IN_FLIGHT, ACCEPTED, UNKNOWN_OUTCOME and FAILED/EXHAUSTED; delivery state independently starts UNKNOWN and becomes CONFIRMED only with verified evidence. Accepted submission remains recorded even without a receipt; absence of confirmation alone does not prove ambiguous send execution or safe fallback. WAHA event/status mapping is unknown until the exact adapter is verified. Confirmation retains evidence and meaning; SMTP handoff is not inbox confirmation.

Before network dispatch, durable intent/attempt identifies what might have been sent. A crash after dispatch creates unknown outcome. Safe retry requires verified idempotency or proof of definite pre-send failure; expiry of a lease alone is insufficient. Late delivery evidence cannot regress confirmed state. Retries are bounded and back off; per-recipient limits and expiry suppress storms.

Default proposal: SMTP fallback on definite terminal WAHA failure; unknown WAHA outcome waits for verified reconciliation or explicit operator policy. Fallback claims lock the logical notification so competing workers cannot both route it. A WAHA late success after SMTP can still duplicate externally; expose both results with one logical event ID. Do not promise exactly-once delivery or silently retry unknown outcomes after restore.

## Freshness, polling and notification objectives — proposed, not provider SLAs

Define source_age(t)=trusted_local_time(t)−source_observation_time at receipt, evaluation and dispatch; malformed/unknown/future-beyond-approved-skew timestamps are ineligible. Source observation time is not a proven time of the economic event/last trade. Poll interval is a nominal 30-minute spacing between admitted slots, distinct from slot start lateness, skipped slots and request/queue duration. Measure evaluation→intent commit→first attempt→ACCEPTED and, only with actual receipt evidence, CONFIRMED delivery. Missing receipt is UNKNOWN, not zero latency.

A quote up to 45 minutes old and up to 30 minutes until the next poll create a conservative age-plus-detection budget up to 75 minutes before processing/outbox delay. This does not promise a 75-minute alert: age is rechecked and old observations may be suppressed, provider visibility can be longer/unknown, quotas/outages can skip polls, and a transient crossing can be entirely missed. Therefore these parameters cannot guarantee notification within 60 minutes of a real event. Neither server took nor a single observed 27–209s sample is a provider SLA. If a <=60-minute experience is mandatory, owner must approve tighter freshness/cadence/capacity and evidence-backed availability requirements; no such guarantee is accepted here.

Proposed healthy best-effort objectives (Q-24): 95% of evaluated quote observations have source age <=5min; 99% of admitted due slots start <=1min late; 95% of eligible committed intents reach adapter ACCEPTED <=2min after commit and <=10min from source timestamp. Their feasibility is not established by the audits (which also publish about30min delay); owner must approve/revise them using later authorized observations, not assume that a provider can meet them. These are internal operating objectives requiring human approval and exact live adapter semantics, not guaranteed provider delay or recipient delivery. The 45min ceiling is a hard proposed eligibility limit, not the 5min objective. Inputs aged 5–45min may be eligible under the approved ceiling but are visibly AGE_DEGRADED and will often breach the source→acceptance objective.

Denominators: count all evaluated observations (rejected/missing timestamps separately, never silently omit); all admitted due slots and separately ALL calendar-due slots with quota/paused/skipped reasons; all eligible committed intents with separate accepted/unknown/failed/expired counts. Report age/latency p50/p95/p99, numerator/denominator and nonaccepted intents as objective failures at deadline, not just successful samples. Evaluate over an approved rolling window (proposal 7 calendar days), report INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE below proposed 30 observations/intents rather than claim compliance. Same timestamp/observation duplicates do not inflate healthy samples. Confirmed-delivery metric is independent, with unknown coverage shown.

States/reasons: HEALTHY, AGE_DEGRADED, POLLING_DEGRADED, QUOTA_DEGRADED, PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE, OUTBOX_DEGRADED, UNKNOWN_TIME, STALE_SUPPRESSED and INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE can coexist per capability. Objective breaches show degraded status and counters; hard age/unknown/stale flag, invalid identity/basis/calendar, paused/quarantined rules or expired intents suppress trigger/rearm/dispatch. Unknown external execution suppresses blind retry. No quota borrowing, fabricated timestamps or silent relaxation to improve metrics. Review oldest pending intent, queue depth, schedule lateness/skips, provider errors/retries, reservations/shared allocation, input age/invalidity, suppressions, acceptance/receipt coverage and objective breaches.
